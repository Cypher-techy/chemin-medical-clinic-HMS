# Data protection (Kenya)
Designed to support — not by itself achieve — compliance with Data Protection Act 2019, General Regulations 2021, Digital Health Act 2023 & applicable MOH/KMPDC/ODPC requirements. Controls: data minimisation (ID number optional/only where necessary), consent records (`Consent`), purpose-bound RBAC, audit & disclosure logging, encryption in transit (TLS) & at rest (managed Postgres/local disk encryption), patient rights workflows via Records Officer (access/correction requests logged as audit events), retention/disposal & DPIA are clinic governance processes the clinic must complete with its DPO; no DHA/SHA certification is claimed. Cross-border AI/external transfers disabled by default.
